Skill Development Curriculum

A 2-year, 16-module program for diploma & degree students — building communication, confidence, financial literacy and true corporate readiness.

>> How the program works

01

Onboarding

Your institution partners with us and students are enrolled into the 2-year, 16-module program alongside their regular academic calendar.

02

Structured Delivery

16 core modules delivered in 4-hour sessions — covering communication, aptitude, financial literacy, entrepreneurship and leadership.

03

Practice & Certification

Students go through mock GDs, interviews and tests, mental health & wellness sessions, and receive certification on completion.

At a glance

  • Communication & public speaking
  • Aptitude & logical thinking
  • Financial literacy & investments
  • Entrepreneurship & leadership
  • Mock GDs, interviews & tests
  • Mental health & wellness
  • Certification on completion
