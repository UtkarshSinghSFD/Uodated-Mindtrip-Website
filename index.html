Skill Development Curriculum

A 2-year, 16-module program for diploma & degree students — building communication, confidence, financial literacy and true corporate readiness.

>> How the program works

01

Onboarding

Your institution partners with us and students are enrolled into the 2-year, 16-module program alongside their regular academic calendar.

02

Structured Delivery

16 core modules delivered in 4-hour sessions — covering communication, aptitude, financial literacy, entrepreneurship and leadership.

03

Practice & Certification

Students go through mock GDs, interviews and tests, mental health & wellness sessions, and receive certification on completion.

At a glance

  • 16 core modules, 4-hour sessions
  • Communication & public speaking
  • Aptitude & logical thinking
  • Financial literacy & investments
  • Entrepreneurship & leadership
  • Mock GDs, interviews & tests
  • Mental health & wellness
  • Certification on completion

MindTripE MusicVerse

A complete music ecosystem inside your school — structured curriculum, instruments, performances and internationally recognized certification pathways.

>> How the program works

01

Setup at Your School

We set up the music lab and acoustics, and provide instruments & books — at zero cost to the school.

02

10-Level Curriculum

Students progress through a 10-level curriculum starting from the nursery & primary program, preparing for Trinity, Yamaha and other grade exams.

03

Performance & Exposure

Students get recording studio exposure and take part in band competitions and live shows.

  • Instruments & books provided
  • Music lab setup & acoustics

  • Recording studio exposure
  • Band competitions & live shows
  • Nursery & primary program
  • Zero cost to school
